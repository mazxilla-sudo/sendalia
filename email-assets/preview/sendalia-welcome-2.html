Your Welcome Email Is A First Date.

Hi {{ first_name|default:'there' }},

Someone just found your brand, liked it enough to hand over their email, and is waiting to see what happens next. Most brands open with "Here's 10% off. Buy now."

That's proposing on the first date. Sometimes it works. Usually it gets you ghosted. The best welcome flows take their time, and they earn the yes.

The story arc we build

Three Emails. Three Jobs.

The structure behind every welcome flow we design for founder-led brands.

01

The Hello

Keep the promise you made on your signup form, then say hi like a human. Who are you, why should they care, and what happens next?

02

The Why

Your founder story. The kitchen table, the late nights, the reason your product exists. This is the email people forward to friends.

03

The Proof

Now you've earned the ask. Best-sellers, real reviews and real faces, then make buying feel easy.
