Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

We tell stories through email for a living, so we know how much trust it takes for someone to hand over their inbox. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

In this policy, "Sendalia", "we", "us" and "our" mean Sendalia, the email marketing studio behind getsendalia.com. If you have any questions, write to us at hello@getsendalia.com.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to do the work you asked for.

  • Newsletter signups. When you join the Sendalia Letters, we collect your email address and, if you choose to share it, your first name.
  • Enquiries and free welcome flow requests. When you fill in one of our forms, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, brand name, website, social handles and details about your brand and goals.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us directly, we keep the conversation so we can reply and follow up.
  • Website and email activity. Our site uses Klaviyo's onsite script, which sets cookies to recognise returning visitors, show our signup form, and measure which pages are viewed. Our emails may record when they are opened or a link is clicked, so we can understand what is useful and send less of what is not.
  • Client account access. If you hire us, you may give us access to tools such as Klaviyo or Shopify. We use that access only to deliver the agreed work.

How we use it

  • To send you the newsletter and other emails you signed up for
  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare your free welcome flow, audit or proposal
  • To deliver, invoice and support client projects
  • To understand how our site and emails perform so we can improve them
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Ever.

The tools we rely on

Like most small studios, we use trusted third-party services to run Sendalia. They process information on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • Klaviyo for our email list, signup forms and onsite tracking
  • Jotform for enquiry and brief forms
  • Google Workspace for email and documents
  • GitHub Pages to host this website
  • PayPal and Stripe to process payments (we never see or store your full card details)

Some of these providers store data outside the country you live in. Each has its own privacy commitments, which we encourage you to read.

Client data we handle

When we work on a client's email program, we may see that brand's customer data inside their own platforms. That data belongs to our client. We act only on their instructions, use it solely to deliver the agreed work, never copy it into our own systems, and give up access when the engagement ends.

How long we keep it

Newsletter subscribers stay on our list until they unsubscribe. Enquiry details are kept for as long as they are useful for the conversation, and no longer than two years without contact. Client and billing records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email we send.
  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to or limit how we use it.
  • Manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking them may stop the signup form from showing.

Email hello@getsendalia.com and we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act or US state privacy laws may give you additional rights, and we will respect them. You can also contact your local data protection authority if you are unhappy with how we have handled your information.

Keeping it safe

We use secure, reputable tools, protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and limit access to the two of us who run Sendalia. No system is perfect, but we treat your information with the same care we would want for our own.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we will let subscribers know by email.

Get in touch

Questions, requests or concerns are always welcome at hello@getsendalia.com.
