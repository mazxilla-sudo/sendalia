Client Spotlight

NŌLA Candles, New Zealand

Small batch soy candles and home fragrance, hand poured in the Wairarapa.

NŌLA started with a simple idea: candles made slowly, in small batches, by people who actually watch each one get poured, cooled, and labelled by hand. No mass production, no shortcuts, just soy wax, cotton wicks, and time. That care shows up in everything they make, from Cabin Fever's whiskey and tobacco leaf warmth to Bouquet's soft white florals, and it's the same care their customers keep coming back for.

We got to build two welcome flows for them: one for new customers discovering NŌLA for the first time, and one for retailers bringing NŌLA onto their shelves. For customers, that meant writing something that feels less like a sales funnel and more like an invitation, a warm hello, a candle care ritual guide, and a rewards program that only shows up once real trust has been built. For wholesale partners, it meant answering the practical questions stockists actually have (margins, lead times, support) before they even have to ask.

Working with NŌLA was the kind of project we love most. Every line had to sound like it came from someone who genuinely hand pours candles in the Wairarapa, not a marketing team writing about one. That's exactly the kind of brief that keeps us honest.

Our Core Positioning

The difference between
storytelling and blasting

Most brands treat email as a broadcast channel. We treat it as an ongoing conversation — one that builds loyalty, drives repeat revenue, and never burns your list.

The Old Way — Promo Blasting

  • Batch-and-blast campaigns every week

    Trains your list to ignore you — or worse, mark you as spam.

  • Generic discount-led subject lines

    Destroys brand equity and conditions customers to wait for sales only.

  • No segmentation or lifecycle logic

    First-time buyers receive the same email as your most loyal customers.

  • Revenue spikes, then vanishes

    Dependent on constant manual effort with no compounding return.

The Sendalia Way — Story-Driven Retention

  • Automated, narrative-led sequences

    Flows that run 24/7 and generate revenue while you sleep.

  • Brand-voice email copy that builds community

    Customers feel heard, not sold to — leading to higher LTV.

  • Deep segmentation & lifecycle strategy

    Every subscriber receives the right message at the right moment.

  • Compounding, predictable retention revenue

    Email becomes your most reliable and scalable revenue channel.
